How this is measured. There is no time tracking, so hours are estimated to the nearest half hour from first and last activity each day — Slack timestamps, calendar events, Claude sessions, downloads and file writes — and are accurate to about ±1 hour a day. Exports and audio bounces are not estimated: every one is a real file on the drive with a real clock time, read from 8 EXPORTS and 8 AUDIOS. Task counts and due dates are pulled live from ClickUp at build time. Commits by Brixton, Morgan and Hazel are not counted as Mick’s hours; they show up only as inbound reading load.
